Gmail

Connect the business's Gmail

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification

    In the Gmail account: Google Account → Security → 2-Step Verification. App passwords need it.

  2. Create an app password

    Google Account → Security → App passwords. Name it "WhatsApp assistant" and copy the 16 letters Google shows. It is not the normal Gmail password.

  3. Paste both here and save

    Then press Send a test email: it goes to this Gmail address.

The 16 letters Google shows, with or without the spaces.
