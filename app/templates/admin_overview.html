First-time setup

The bot is running. This is the order to wire it up — each step links where you'll click.

  1. On the server, put the Google service-account key at secrets/google-service-account.json (in the Whatsapp-assistant folder), then share the business's Google Sheet with the key's client_email as Editor. The bot reads prices and writes orders through it.
  2. Add a business — web id, business and bot names, time zone, and the Sheet's id (the long code in the Sheet's web address). Press Check Sheet access; if it lists your tabs, step 1 worked.
  3. Open the business → Sheet: pick the Knowledge and Handoffs tabs, and set what customers may read or add on each tab.
  4. Staff & groups: paste staff WhatsApp numbers (with country code) — they save rows without confirmation; everyone else is a customer.
  5. Official number: paste the client's Meta token, app secret and phone-number id. Then set the webhook URL and verify token shown there in the client's Meta app, subscribing to messages. Meta also needs a payment method on the client's WhatsApp account.
  6. Numbers (top bar): add a purchased number and scan the QR with the spare phone, then add it to the client's WhatsApp groups.
  7. Send the official number a message and watch it answer. Point a free monitor at /health so you know if it ever goes down.

The full click-by-click guide lives in the repo: docs/setup-guide.md.
