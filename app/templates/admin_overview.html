Set up your first client

  1. Share the Sheet with the bot

  2. Add the business →

    Name, bot name, time zone, and the Sheet id — the long code in the Sheet's web address, between /d/ and /edit. Then press Check Sheet access: if your tabs appear, this step works.

  3. Pick the tabs the bot may use

    Inside the business → Sheet: choose the Knowledge and Handoffs tabs, and set what customers may read or add on each tab.

  4. Add staff

    Staff & groups → paste staff WhatsApp numbers with the country code. Staff save rows instantly; customers confirm with YES.

Then

Connect WhatsApp numbers

  1. Official number — customer chats

    Business → Official number: paste the client's Meta token, app secret and phone-number id. Set the webhook URL and verify token shown there inside the client's Meta app (subscribe to messages), and add a payment method in the client's WhatsApp account.

  2. Group number — group @mentions

    Numbers in the top bar → add a purchased number → scan the QR with the spare phone → add it to the client's groups. Keep that phone online at least every 14 days.

Finally

Go live

  1. Test it

    Message the official number a question that's answered by the Sheet — the reply should arrive in seconds. Point a free monitor (e.g. UptimeRobot) at /health so you know if it ever goes down.
